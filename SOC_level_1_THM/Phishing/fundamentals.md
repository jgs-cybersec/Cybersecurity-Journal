# Fundamentals of Phishing

Spam and Phishing remain the most common social engineering threats facing modern organizations. While **spam is often low-risk**, /**phishing can trick users into disclosing sensitive information or unknowingly deploying malware**/.

Every Phishing investigation starts with analyzing E-mail address- 

## Anatomy Of an E-mail.

An email is composed of the following elements:

1. Username: User mailbox that identifies the specific recipient’s mailbox on the email server -----> david
2. @ symbol: Separates the username from the domain and tells the system where to route the email ------> @
3. Domain name: Specifies the mail server responsible for receiving the message -------> gmail.com

## Email delivery protocol

SMTP: Simple Mail Transfer Protocol -----> Sends email
POP3: Post Office Protocol -----> Downloads email
IMAP: Internet Message Access Protocol -----> Sync email in different devices.
