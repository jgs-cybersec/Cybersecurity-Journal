# **SOC Analyst(Junior Security Analyst)**

SOC team (24/7 working team) Includes-
1. Junior sec Analyst
2. Senior Analyst
3. SOC Engg
4. Incident responder
5. SOC Manager

Therefore this is a team work not an individual work.

## SOC==Security Operations Center

Team aims to continuously monitor an organization’s network and resources and identify suspicious activity to prevent damage. This team works 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

**SOC Team---->Detect---->Respond**

Detection of -------> Vulnerability
             -------> Unauthorized access
             -------> Policy Violation
             -------> Intrusion (etc-The employee accessing any malicious site and getting affected)

Response with the incident response team.

Three pillars of SOC is-
1.**People : The SOC team** automated detection and responding may not be that effective. Therefore need a human to perform for the true detection and take action
2.Process
3.Technology


There are many open-source databases out there, like **AbuseIPDB, and Cisco Talos Intelligence**, where you can perform a reputation and location check for the IP address.
