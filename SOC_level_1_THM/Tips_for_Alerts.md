# Tips for Alert Monitoring


- C:\Users\Public\revshell.exe

  **highly critical cyberattack** in progress. The name **revshell.exe is shorthand for Reverse Shell**, a malicious tool used by hackers to bypass firewalls and gain remote control of a compromised computer.


- SPF/Fail; DKIM/Fail;

  Status means the incoming email completely failed both primary email authentication checks. This is a massive security alert indicating that the email is highly likely to be spoofed, a phishing attempt, or malicious.
