# SOC_Workbook

Essential for a L1 SOC Analyst to perform analysis of the new alerts that pop ups. 

## Inventory

There are two types of inventories-
1. Identity Inventory: Records the details of the user, role, mialID etc. {BambooHR, Active Directory}
2. Assest Inventory: Details about the Host, it's location etc.

## Network Diagram

This is the diagram Which shows the entire network of the company. From this Diagram the Analyst can collect the details of IPs, subnets etc. 

## Workbook

This is a guideline for the L1 employees for better working directions. It explains about how to ***enrich*** about the details from the situation/alert happened. 
Then **investigate** the alert on the basis of the details collected and take actions.
The actions includes-**Escalation** or making the alert as False Alert.
