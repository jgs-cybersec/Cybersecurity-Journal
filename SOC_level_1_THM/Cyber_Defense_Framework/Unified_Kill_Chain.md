# Unified Kill Chain

 a “Kill Chain” is used to describe the methodology/path attackers such as hackers or APTs use to approach and intrude a target.

 For example, an attacker scanning, exploiting a web vulnerability, and escalating privileges will be a “Kill Chain”.

 ## Threat Modeling

 Threat modelling is an important procedure in reducing the risk within a system or application, as it creates a high-level overview of an organisation's IT assets and the procedures to resolve vulnerabilities.
 STRIDE, DREAD and CVSS (to name a few) are all frameworks specifically used in threat modelling.

 ## Benefits of the Unified Kill Chain (UKC) Framework

 - Modern (released in 2017, updated in 2022).
 - The UKC is extremely detailed (18 phases).
 - The UKC covers an entire attack - from reconnaissance, exploitation, post-exploitation and includes identifying an attacker's motivation.
 - The UKC highlights a much more **realistic attack scenario**. Various stages will often re-occur. For example, after exploiting a machine, an attacker will begin reconnaissance to pivot another system.
