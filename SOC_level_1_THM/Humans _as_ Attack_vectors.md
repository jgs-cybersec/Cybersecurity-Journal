# Humans as Attack Vectors

Humans-----> The **weakest link in cyber security** and those who help threat actors the most.

Instead of taking time for bypassing firewall defenses, attackers easy way to get access is phishing one of the employee on the network.

## Why Humans Are Targeted

Humans are targeted because of the **access** they can provide - to websites, mailboxes, or databases.

Examples:-
1. Breach Google account of the HR manager ------> Steal and sell the entire employee database.
2. Trick a wealthy person into running malware-------> Hijack a web banking session from their PC.
3. Breach the IT administrator's VPN account -------> Access the heart of a big corporate network.
4. Trick a government worker into sharing secrets -------> Use the information to simplify the next attacks.
